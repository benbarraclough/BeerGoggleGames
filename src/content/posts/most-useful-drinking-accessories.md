A list of accessories that will assist you with some creative drinking methods.

A good collection of drinking equipment can add some flair to your drinking, adding something special to how you prepare or consume your drinks.

The following items aren't sponsored, the links are just examples of where the items can be found. There are loads of websites where you can get the same item but could change in price and quality.

#### Beer Bong

An undeniable favourite for consuming large amounts of alcohol in a short amount of time.

This drinking apparatus utilises three vital components, the funnel, the pipe and the valve to hold, deliver and control the consumption of whatever you put in there.

A beer bong can be used for forfeits, races and getting those who haven't drank as much caught up with everyone else. Find yourself a decent size with tubing thats at least 2 feet long so that it can be used properly.

Struggle to neck your bottled drinks? Being called a pussy by your friends? Do we have the solution for you!

The drinking snorkel is engineered to deliver alcohol from bottle to mouth so you can finish the drink without air-pressure interrupting you.

A good way to quickly drink from a bottle and even get a full set and race your mates.

#### Cap Shooter

A brilliant way to maintain a tidy space and keep the drinks flowing in at the same time whilst sat on your arse.

Use the gun as a part of a game by necking drinks and shooting targets or keep it yourself to issue punishments, your choice.

#### Spirit Pistol

A fun way to get shots down during a party.

Fill the gun up with whatever spirit and give it a squirt. To get a full shot out of a pistol, probably needs a few squirts, so be prepared to refill quite often if its a small one.

Use this as a punishment during games or pass a few round for a bit of fun during a night of drinking.

#### Shotgun Tool

The tool for all the pro drinkers out there who love to shotgun a beer... more info on shotguns in the [glossary](extras/glossary).

One of the best ways to drain a can is a good shotgun, but the main problem for most is getting the hole in the can without destroying it completely or shaking the beer up so much that its all foam.

This is a tool that makes it as easy as a bottle opener, just press, pierce and chug. Can be easily attached to keys so that a shotgun is always on the cards.
