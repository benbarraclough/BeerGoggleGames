<GameSection title="Requirements">

- Beer Pong table
- Beer pong balls
- solo cups
- Score table
- A champions prize

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play">

Begin by splitting the players into teams of 2 and making a bracket to determine who plays who.

Bracket template below:

![](/images/brackets.webp)

The first two teams to face off start a regular game of [Beer Pong](/games/beer-pong/). Once over, the win is recorded on the table and the losing team is knocked out, winners moving to the next round closer to the final.

This proceeds until only 2 teams are left who will then play each other for the championship prize. The game takes a while so reducing the amount of cups on either side can help speed things up.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Top Tips">

- Use a trophy or a plaque to give as the championship prize.
- If there are a lot of teams, have multiple tables set up.
- Use a group stage points table with a quick pong minigame to decide who places where on the bracket.

</GameSection>
