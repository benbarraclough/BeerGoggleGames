<GameSection title="How to Play">

This game is versatile enough to be played with any number of players, but the smaller the group the better. Playing alone isn’t really recommended but if you feel the need, go ahead.

Find a buddy or two to join in on the fun, decide on your weapon of choice: beer, spirits, wine, or whatever’s hiding in the back of the fridge. You can team up and see how long you can keep your cover intact, or go full-on rivalry mode to see who gets found out first, seeing who can chug the most without spilling the beans.

Once someone gets caught, the other players can either come clean or keep the deception going until they’re the next to be busted.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Top Tips">
<ul>
<li>Find a safe place where the alcohol can be stored or hidden so you don't have to drink it nearby the others.</li>
<li>Use breath mints so that alcohol can't be smelt on your breath.</li>
<li>Have a water on the side used for either a decoy or is actually filled with a bit of vodka too.</li>
<li>This is a dangerous game to be good at, so be careful how you play.</li>
</ul>
</GameSection>
