  <p><strong>Description:</strong> A smooth and creamy after-dinner cocktail mixing cognac with chocolate and a little nutmeg.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 3 mins</p>

  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Cocktail glass</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> Contains dairy</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> Early 20th century</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Cognac or brandy - 30ml</li>
    <li>Brown crème de cacao - 30ml</li>
    <li>Fresh cream - 30ml</li>
    <li>Freshly grated nutmeg (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Add the brandy, crème de cacao and cream to a shaker with ice.</li>
    <li>Shake well until the mixture is properly chilled.</li>
    <li>Strain into a chilled cocktail glass.</li>
    <li>Grate a little fresh nutmeg over the top.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Freshly grated nutmeg makes a big difference to the finished drink.</li>
    <li>For a richer version, use double cream, but shake well to combine.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
