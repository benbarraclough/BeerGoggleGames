  <p><strong>Description:</strong> A sweet, fruity, and refreshing cocktail combining a mix of liqueurs and orange juice.</p>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Southern Comfort - 25ml</li>
    <li>Amaretto - 25ml</li>
    <li>Sloe Gin - 25ml</li>
    <li>Orange Juice - 50ml</li>
    <li>Ice - 1 handful</li>
    <li>Maraschino Cherry - 1 (optional)</li>
    <li>Orange wedge - 1 (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Add liqueurs and orange juice into a shaker with some ice.</li>
    <li>Shake and strain into glass with more ice.</li>
    <li>Garnish with a cherry and an orange wedge.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Juice your own oranges rather than using store bought juice from a carton.</li>
    <li>Add the orange wedge and maraschino cherry as a garnish.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
