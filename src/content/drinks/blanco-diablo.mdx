<DrinkSection title="Overview" icon="/images/info.webp">
  <p><strong>Description:</strong> A creamy, citrusy absinthe cocktail with falernum, lemon and a foamy egg-white finish.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 4 mins</p>
  <p><strong>Difficulty:</strong> Medium</p>
  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Cocktail glass</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> Contains dairy and egg; check falernum ingredients</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> Modern cocktail</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Absinthe - 30ml</li>
    <li>Falernum syrup - 22.5ml</li>
    <li>Fresh lemon juice - 15ml</li>
    <li>Double cream - 15ml</li>
    <li>Egg white - 1</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Add all the ingredients to a cocktail shaker without ice.</li>
    <li>Shake firmly to combine and create a foamy texture.</li>
    <li>Add ice and shake again until cold.</li>
    <li>Strain into a chilled cocktail glass and garnish with a lemon twist if desired.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>This is the absinthe-based Blanco Diablo recipe attributed to bartender Francisco Mora. Other cocktails with this name exist.</li>
    <li>Use pasteurised egg white if you would rather not use raw egg.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
