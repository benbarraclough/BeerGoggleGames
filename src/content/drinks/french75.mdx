<DrinkSection title="Overview" icon="/images/info.webp">
  <p><strong>Description:</strong> Gin, lemon and Champagne come together in a bright, fizzy cocktail that packs more punch than it looks.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 4 mins</p>
  <p><strong>Difficulty:</strong> Medium</p>
  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Champagne flute</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> Contains sulphites (Champagne)</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> France</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Gin - 30ml</li>
    <li>Fresh lemon juice - 15ml</li>
    <li>Sugar syrup - 15ml</li>
    <li>Champagne - 60ml</li>
    <li>Lemon twist (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Add the gin, lemon juice and sugar syrup to a shaker filled with ice.</li>
    <li>Shake until cold and strain into a chilled Champagne flute.</li>
    <li>Slowly top with chilled Champagne and give it a gentle stir.</li>
    <li>Garnish with a lemon twist if you have one.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Never shake the Champagne; add it after straining to keep the bubbles.</li>
    <li>A dry sparkling wine works if you don't have Champagne.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
