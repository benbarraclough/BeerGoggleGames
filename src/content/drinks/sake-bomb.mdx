<DrinkSection title="Overview" icon="/images/info.webp">
  <p><strong>Description:</strong> An easy-drinking Japanese party trick cocktail thats quick, simple and fun for groups.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 3 mins</p>
  <p><strong>Difficulty:</strong> Medium</p>
  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Pint glass</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> None</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> Japan</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
  <li>Lager - 300-400ml</li>
  <li>Sake - 50ml</li>
  <li>Chopsticks - 1 pair</li>
</ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
  <li>Add the lager to the pint glass, making sure that it fills maybe 3/4 of the way.</li>
  <li>Separate the two chopsticks and place them flat on top of the pint glass</li>
  <li>Fill a shot glass with sake and place this on top of the pint glass, balancing in between the two chopsticks.</li>
  <li>When you're ready, smash on the table and watch the shot glass fall into the pint of lager, then drink.</li>
</ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Balancing the shot glass at an angle can help with stability.</li>
    <li>If you're in a group, try setting up multiple and smashing the table at the same time, or compete in a race to set up and drop your shot glass in cleanly.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
