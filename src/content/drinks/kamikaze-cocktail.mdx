<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Vodka - 30ml</li>
    <li>Triple sec - 30ml</li>
    <li>Fresh lime juice - 30ml</li>
    <li>Lime twist (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Add vodka, triple sec and fresh lime juice to a shaker with ice.</li>
    <li>Shake well until cold.</li>
    <li>Strain into a chilled cocktail glass and garnish with a lime twist if you fancy it.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>The equal-parts version is particularly sharp. Add a little sugar syrup if you prefer it sweeter.</li>
    <li>For the shorter, vodka-forward version, try our Kamikaze shot recipe.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
