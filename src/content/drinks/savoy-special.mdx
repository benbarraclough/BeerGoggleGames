<DrinkSection title="Overview" icon="/images/info.webp">
  <p><strong>Description:</strong> A strong, old-school gin cocktail with dry vermouth, a touch of grenadine and a dash of absinthe.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 3 mins</p>
  <p><strong>Difficulty:</strong> Medium</p>
  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Cocktail glass</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> Vermouth generally contains sulphites</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> United Kingdom</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>London dry gin - 60ml</li>
    <li>Dry vermouth - 30ml</li>
    <li>Grenadine - 5ml</li>
    <li>Absinthe - 1 dash</li>
    <li>Lemon twist (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Add gin, dry vermouth, grenadine and absinthe to a shaker filled with ice.</li>
    <li>Shake thoroughly and fine strain into a chilled cocktail glass.</li>
    <li>Squeeze a lemon twist over the surface and use it as a garnish.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>This is the Savoy Hotel Special No. 1 recipe, adapted from The Savoy Cocktail Book.</li>
    <li>Go easy on the absinthe; one dash is enough to give it an aniseed edge.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
