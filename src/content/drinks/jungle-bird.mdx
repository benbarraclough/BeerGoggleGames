<DrinkSection title="Overview" icon="/images/info.webp">
  <p><strong>Description:</strong> A tropical mix of dark rum, pineapple and lime with a bitter edge from Campari.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 4 mins</p>
  <p><strong>Difficulty:</strong> Medium</p>
  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Rocks glass</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> None specified; check individual products</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> Malaysia</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Blackstrap rum - 45ml</li>
    <li>Campari - 22.5ml</li>
    <li>Pineapple juice - 45ml</li>
    <li>Fresh lime juice - 15ml</li>
    <li>Demerara sugar syrup - 15ml</li>
    <li>Pineapple wedge (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>Pour the rum, Campari, pineapple juice, lime juice and syrup into a shaker with ice.</li>
    <li>Shake thoroughly until chilled.</li>
    <li>Strain into a rocks glass filled with fresh ice.</li>
    <li>Add a pineapple wedge to garnish.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>The Campari adds bitterness, so don't skip it if you want the proper balance.</li>
    <li>Demerara syrup brings richer sweetness than standard sugar syrup.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
