<DrinkSection title="Overview" icon="/images/info.webp">
  <p><strong>Description:</strong> A sharp, refreshing tequila classic with fresh lime and orange liqueur. The salted rim is up to you.</p>
  <p><strong>Time:</strong> 3 mins</p>
  <p><strong>Difficulty:</strong> Easy</p>
  <p><strong>Glass type:</strong> Cocktail or margarita glass</p>
  <p><strong>Dietary Notes:</strong> None</p>
  <p><strong>Origin:</strong> Mexico</p>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Ingredients" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Tequila (100% agave) - 50ml</li>
    <li>Triple sec - 20ml</li>
    <li>Fresh lime juice - 15ml</li>
    <li>Salt for the rim (optional)</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Recipe" icon="/images/rules.webp">
  <ol>
    <li>If using salt, run a lime wedge around half the glass rim and dip it into a little salt.</li>
    <li>Add the tequila, triple sec and freshly squeezed lime juice to a shaker with ice.</li>
    <li>Shake well and strain into a chilled cocktail glass.</li>
    <li>Garnish with a lime wedge if you fancy it.</li>
  </ol>
</DrinkSection>

<DrinkSection title="Tips" icon="/images/lightbulb.webp">
  <ul>
    <li>Use fresh lime rather than bottled juice for the best result.</li>
    <li>For a sweeter Margarita, add a little sugar syrup to taste.</li>
  </ul>
</DrinkSection>
