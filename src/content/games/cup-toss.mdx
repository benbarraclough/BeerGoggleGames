<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   Solo Cups
-   Beer Bottles
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Split into two teams, providing each player with two beer bottles and a cup. Teams should be standing 8 feet apart facing each-other and players should spread out so there is enough room to place 2 bottles of beer by their feet, visible to the other team.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Toss the solo cup so it lands on top of the oppositions beer bottle to knock them out and make them drink.</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
The starting team begins by throwing their cup(s) and attempting to land them over the top of one of the oppositions bottles.

Once all cups have been thrown by the team, the cups can be picked up and thrown by the opposing team.

If a cup successfully lands on a bottle without knocking it over. The player who’s drink it is must finish it at the end of that round.

The game continues until a team loses all of its bottles. The remaining bottles on the winning team must then be drank by the losers.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   Knees must be stright when throwing.
-   Neither cups or bottles cannot be interfered with during a round.
-   If a cup lands on a bottle and is knocked off or falls off before the end of the round, it does not count.
-   If a bottle is smashed, it needs to be replaced.
-   If a player has no bottles left in the game they can no longer throw.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Circle toss off</h4>

No teams needed, it’s a free for all. Everyone gets in a circle with their own two beer bottles at their feet. 

With two or three cups in play, each player in the group must try and throw the cup onto another’s cup bottle to make them drink. Once both players’ bottles are gone, the player is knocked out. The last player remaining wins and gets to hand their remaining bottle(s) to another player.
</GameSection>
