<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   2-3 Metal Darts
-   A can each
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Position all players equal distance away from each other, either on chairs or standing with their cans at their feet. Assign one player as the thrower to begin who will start with all the darts.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Penetrate an opponents can by throwing darts at it, making them finish their drink before you.</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
The game begins by the thrower choosing a players can to aim for and throwing all darts at the target in an attempt to penetrate the can.

If a dart makes contact with a player's can but does not puncture it, that player must take one sip of beer from their own separate, personal drink.

If a dart punctures a player's can, that player must drink from the can to at least the hole stopping any leaks.

If a dart strikes a player, the thrower must finish their own drink.

If a can is punctured three times, or if a dart enters the mouth of a can, that beer must be fully consumed.

Once all darts have been thrown, the player who has had their can shot at can now take their turn.

This continues until there is only 1 player with a can remaining.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   Players can use backboards to shield themselves from the darts
-   When a can is penetrated, it must be drank there and then to stop the leak before play can resume
-   Cans must be unopened until hit or penetrated
-   Darts should be removed from a can when penetrated to be used again
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Extreme Beer Darts</h4>

-   Double the distance between players
-   Play with multiple cans each
-   Players can use beer mats to block incoming darts from hitting their cans
-   Free for all throwing: Players can throw darts anywhere at any time
</GameSection>
