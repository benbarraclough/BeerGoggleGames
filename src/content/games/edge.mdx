<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   Dice
-   A table
-   A drink each

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Gather everyone at one end of a table with a full drink and a dice each, try and use dice that different from eachother if possible to tell them apart.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Roll your individual die closest to the end of the table, beating other players to make them drink.</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
The order of play is decided at the start of each round by rolling dice to see who gets the highest number, the player with the highest number gets to choose the order. Players then take it in turns to roll their dice down to the end of the table in attempt to get as close to the edge as they can.

Whichever player gets their die closest to the end of the table is the winner and gets to dish out the amount of sips designated on the die to other players, (e.g. 5 on the dice is 5 sips, divided however you like)

The players who land their dice on the table but don't get closest to the edge have no forfeit.

Any player who rolls their die off of the table surface must drink 3 sips.

This continues until one person wins 3 times.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   All players should roll from the same point.
-   No roll interference is allowed.
-   If multiple dice are equally as close to the edge, those players can either take a reroll without any other players involved.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Obstacles</h4>

<p>Place beer bottles or other objects on the playing surface to act as obstacles making it harder to roll the dice to the edge of the table. Assign a forfeit for when an obstacle is hit.</p>
</GameSection>
