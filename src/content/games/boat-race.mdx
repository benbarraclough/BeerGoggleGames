<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Once everyones drinks are equally filled to the top, get into even teams (the longer the boat the better). Each team then sits in a row one behind the other like you're in a boat (as shown in the pic). Then await the start of the race.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Work as a team to finish your drinks before the opposing team(s)</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
To start the race, everyone must be in their teams sat down with a full drink in hand.

A countdown begins and each team starts on "go", the first player must chug their drink until finished and then put the cup on their head to signal to the next player in the boat to drink. Each player only drinking once the person in front of them has finished.

Once all players have finished their drinks, the whole team must quickly hold their cups on top of their head before any other team to win.

Each losing team must finish their drinks and perform a forfeit chosen by the winning team
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   To finish, each team must have their cup on their head
-   All drinks must be equal amounts
-   You can't drink until the previous player finishes their drink
-   Shouting and making as much noise as possible is necessary
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Big Jugs</h4>

<p>Instead of having a cup for each player, there will only be one big jug per team. The team must each drink a portion of their jug and pass it to the player behind them. Which is finished by the player at the back of the boat</p>
</GameSection>
