<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   13 or 21 solo cups
-   1 pong ball
-   A table
-   Booze
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Split into 2 teams, each taking 6 or 10 cups (based on size of game) and either fill them halfway with water or booze, positioning them in a triangle formation at the end of the table pointing towards eachother. Fill 1 more cup with water as a wash cup, placing it in the centre of the table to the side.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>First team to score the ball in all of the oppositions cups first wins.</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
The starting player from each team must play rock paper scissors to decide which team throws first.

Each team then takes it in turn to shoot the one ball from their end of the table to the oppositions cups at the other end.

If the thrower scores the shot, the cup that was scored in is removed from the rack and placed on the side. The team that loses the cup must finish the drink in the cup or all take 2 sips of their drink.

If the thrower misses the shot, the thrower is shamed and then the next team gets to throw.

Once a team’s last cup is hit, they get a Redemption round. They have as many shots as there are cups left on the table to try and clear the board. If they sink them all, the game is saved—the lost cups are refilled, and the match continues into overtime. If not, thats game over.

The losing team must then finish the rest of the drinks on the table or a forfeit drink.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   Each team gets 1 throw at a time
-   When throwing, your elbow must be behind the table
-   To score, the ball must touch the water
-   If you spill your own cup, you lose the cup
-   Each team gets 1 reshuffle of the other teams cup rack
-   The defending team cannot interfere with throws
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Hardcore Rules</h4>

-   If you throw the ball and catch the rebound or rollback without it touching the floor, you get a bonus throw
-   If the ball hits any other cup before going in another cup, all cups count and are removed
-   If the ball hits one of your cups and you catch it with one hand, you can take one of the oppositions cups away
-   Combo: If you score your ball in the oppositions cup, you retain possession of the ball until you miss
-   If you throw the ball into a cup that you have already scored in, it is added back to the oppositions rack
-   If the ball bounces when thrown it can be swiped away to defend cups
-   If you score a bouncing throw, you can choose another cup to remove
-   If the ball is spinning round the rim, it can be blown out using only air (no touch)
</GameSection>
