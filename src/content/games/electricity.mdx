<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Gather all players into a circle with their drinks filled and deal cards out evenly between everyone face down.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Drink as a group by playing your cards and forming connections by matching with other players around the circle.</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
1 by 1 moving clockwise around the circle, each player flips over and reveals their top card to the group. Depending on the card, this may create a chain by matching a previous players card.

Conductors: Matching suits and card values

Example play:
Player 1 - Ace♤
Player 2 - 4♤ (matching suit)
Player 3 - 4♧ (matching number)
Player 4 - J♡ (no match, chain ends)

If you flip a card that matches the previous players card, a chain is created and drinking begins for those players in the chain. Cards continue to be flipped by the following players in quick succession with the same rules, if you match, you join the chain and start to drink. Drinks shouldn't be dropped until the chain is broken.

The game continues in a circle as fast as you want, creating and breaking chains. This ends when there are no cards left to flip and can be played again.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   If you're in an active chain, you must be drinking.
-   No peaking at your cards, blind flips only.
-   No delayed card flips.
-   No pretend drinking.
-   The number 8 will always bridge a connection whether it matches or not.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Breaker</h4>

<p>Instead of players drinking when they make a connection, the player who kills the connection must drink the amount of sips that is on their card. This slows drinking down between the group but ends up making less people drink more.</p>
</GameSection>
