<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   52 Card deck
-   A drink each
-   a table

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
All cards are dealt out evenly between the players face down on the table so that they cannot be seen.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Be quick to call other players a dick head before they call you, the quickest drinks the least</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
Still having cards facing down so they can't be seen, all players must pick up their cards. Then simultaneously all players flip the top card over and place it face up on the table for everyone to see. If your card is 1 above, the same or 1 below the number of another players card, you can call them a "dick head" by pointing at them and shouting.

If someone correctly calls you a dick head before you call them, you must take one sip before the next card is played.

If you correctly call another player a dick head before they call you, they must take one sip before the next card is played.

If someone incorrectly calls someone a dick head, they must take 2 sips themselves.

The round ends when no more dick heads can be called and then everyone flips over the next card. 

The game ends once everyone runs out of cards.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   Everyone must flip their cards at the same time, no delays or else they get a punishment
-   A player can call multiple players a dick head
-   A player cannot call the same person a dickhead more than once in a round
-   A player can be called a dick head by multiple people
-   No slow flips
-   If a decision on which player was quicker cannot be made, everyone must vote
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Survival</h4>

<p>The player who is the last one holding cards wins: Each time you call somebody a dick head, you can take their card and put it to the bottom of your pile giving you an extra life. You cannot take your own card back, this is left on the table. The player who wins gets to give 1 other player a forfeit.</p>
</GameSection>
