<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   Solo Cups
-   Pong Balls
-   A drink each
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
Each team of 3-5 players should be shoulder-to-shoulder facing the opposition and have their cups or 'soldiers' racked in front of them in a triangle filled 3/4 full. 

Balls are then placed in the centre of the table for all to grab, there should be a few more balls than there are players.

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Knock out the other team by scoring balls into their cups.</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
Players start by simultaneously grabbing balls from the centre and throwing them into the other teams cups.

If a cup is scored into, this soldier is must be removed from the battlefield.

If a shot is missed, this ball can be grabbed and re-thrown.

The game continues until only 1 team has remaining.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   Cups must be removed when they have been scored into, if there is a dead cup still in the rack of a player, any shot of theirs is void.
-   Blocking is only allowed if the ball bounces.
-   If a player has no more cups in their rack, they can no longer shoot for their team.
-   Any player can shoot at any time in any cup during the game.
-   Each player must only shoot from behind their rack of cups.
-   A shooters elbows must be behind the edge of the table.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>World War</h4>

<p>Introduce more than 2 teams with a bigger table, more people, more cups and more balls</p>
</GameSection>
