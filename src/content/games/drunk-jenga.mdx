<GameSection title="Equipment" icon="/images/liquor.webp">
-   1 Jenga tower
-   A pen
-   Hard alcohol
-   A drink each
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Setup" icon="/images/settings.webp">
1.  Lay the bricks out on the floor
2.  Assign each brick with a task, rule or question, giving multiple bricks the same task based on the liklihood you would like them to be chosen
3.  Build the tower
4.  All players grab a drink

</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Aim of the Game" icon="/images/target.webp">
<p>Don't topple the tower or else you face the hard alcohol bottle</p>
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="How to Play" icon="/images/question.webp">
Starting with the most tower-like person in the group, take a jenga brick from the tower without toppling the tower and do what it says. If the player refuses, they must pick two more and complete those instead.

Once a task is complete, the next player clockwise around the group must take their turn

If a player makes the tower fall, they must take as many sips as the group decides from the hard alcohol bottle and restack the tower.

Play on until you're sick of pulling bricks.
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Rules" icon="/images/rules.webp">
-   No stabilising the tower when taking the brick
-   No disrupting the tower when its not your turn
-   No peaking at the task and choosing the task you like
</GameSection>

<GameSection title="Special Edition" icon="/images/special.webp">

<h4>Re-construction</h4>

<p>Take 3 bricks from the tower before starting the game and place them next to the original tower together side by side, this will be the foundation of the new tower. Each brick taken off of the original tower will need to be placed onto the new tower, they can be placed in any way the player wants, as long as it doesn't touch the ground/table. If this tower falls they must also drink from the hard alcohol bottle.</p>
</GameSection>
